# Peacock One — Adaptive Search, Answer & Generative Intelligence OS

## Absolute rule

We are **not** trying to build:

> Semrush + an AI dashboard.

We are building:

# PEACOCK ONE

**An Adaptive Search, Answer & Generative Intelligence Operating System.**

## Moat (target)

The moat should eventually be the compound of:

- multi-source evidence  
- probabilistic AI measurement  
- multi-model reasoning  
- citation graph  
- entity intelligence  
- experiments  
- writer intelligence  
- decision optimisation  
- execution  
- proprietary outcome learning  

## Product standard

Do not answer only “How visible are we?”

Answer the full set: certainty, why, compared with whom, sources, entities, losing intents, what to change, highest expected value, who executes, what if we don’t, did it work, what did Peacock learn.
